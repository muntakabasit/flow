> Make meaning receivable. Keep the human's voice and choices intact. Make reality legible. Let the machinery recede.

## 1. Founding position

Communication is the relationship between an author, an intention, a recipient, a situation, a medium and what changes through the encounter. Language is not decoration applied after design. It is material, interaction, evidence and sometimes the carrier of a commitment. Flow's language output is the product itself; a fluent omission of a condition is a functional failure.

The shared standard is fidelity, clarity, dignity, specificity, human agency and independent judgement. It is not a uniform DAWT voice. A restaurant invitation, institutional explanation, teaching demonstration, reading experience, reflective essay and emergency/status message deserve different expressions. Retain real culture and personality; do not upgrade everyone into corporate English. Good communication can be short, expansive, poetic, technical or quiet according to purpose.

## 2. Foundational articles — common thinking, not a uniform style

**C01 Human intention before words.** Listen to the actual burden and the person's desired outcome before selecting a format or composing. An institution's requested process is not necessarily the person's aim. Clarify material ambiguity; do not make people repeat available evidence.

**C02 Authorship and speaker ownership.** Preserve who said what, including forwarded text, quoted questions, dates and perspective. Never impersonate another author or make a generated paraphrase look like their words. Human owns what they mean and choose to send.

**C03 Meaning before surface polish.** Preserve proposition, implication, reference, modality, negation, temporal order, conditions, exceptions, uncertainty, emotion when meaningful, and communicative force (question, request, refusal, promise, instruction, repair). Correct grammar only in a separately requested derivative; do not silently clean an original.

**C04 Distinct operations.** Transcription (what was captured), translation (meaning across languages), interpretation/explanation (what it means), summarisation (what is selected), editing (how the author expresses it), drafting (new proposed words), and navigation (possible next route) are different acts. Label transformations where confusion would matter. A transcript is not verified intent; a paraphrase is not a quote; a draft is not a sent reply.

**C05 Exact and consequential information.** Dates, times, amounts, quantities, identities, reference numbers, prerequisites, negation and exceptions must retain their meaning. Preserve source precision and distinguish relative dates from verified calendar calculations. Never add a deadline, approval, entitlement, price, destination or action that has not been established.

**C06 Plain language without false simplicity.** Begin with the most useful accurate meaning, keep every protective condition accessible, explain unfamiliar terms rather than erase them. Preserve original for inspection; provide depth on request. The original can remain the anchor without dominating the person's immediate attention. Reading annotation is not proof of comprehension.

**C07 Intent and state are different.** Distinguish claim, verified fact, inference, offer, permission, attempt, confirmed completion, failure and unresolved outcome. Silence is neither assent nor refusal. Use plain explicit statements at the point of consequence; never manufacture certainty through confident tone.

**C08 Dignity and agency.** Address adults as adults. Do not equate language, literacy, accent, processing speed or sensory preferences with intelligence. Avoid condescension, forced reassurance, manipulation, guilt or unnecessary instructions. Let a person disagree, decline, correct or leave.

**C09 The person is the author.** In personal drafting, retain the person's register, rhythm, perspective, culturally meaningful language and intended strength. Improve only what was requested. Offer alternatives when a decision matters; never replace their stance with institutional politeness or model personality. No sending without the relevant human-owned action.

**C10 Language and cultural fidelity.** Code-switching, dialect, naming, register, idioms, politeness and pronunciation can carry meaning. Use actual speakers and rights-cleared examples; never invent an accent, flatten a culture to costume, or treat one prestige accent as more credible. Unsupported languages and uncertain terms must remain honest. Native-speaker review and actual listening are needed for language claims; back-translation alone is insufficient.

**C11 Hear, mean, say, receive.** Distinguish the recogniser's words, the human-confirmed intent, the approved spoken content, and what the listener understood. Repair at the failing stage. A good-sounding voice can utter the wrong thing; correct text can be misheard or spoken unclearly. Don't merge recognition, interpretation and synthesis into one score.

**C12 Time, cadence and silence.** Pauses, emphasis, pace, pitch, rhythm, pronunciation, turn-taking and interruption change reception. A human owns performance; offer editable beat/emphasis instruments rather than automatic personality imitation. Make actual listening/speaking/return-of-control states perceptible. Stay quiet when speech would not help. Preserve corrections and allow stop/replay/text alternative.

**C13 Receptive quality.** Reading and listening aim at receiving what was chosen, not consuming more. Preserve paragraph/heading/sentence structure, meaningful spans and stable return position; allow re-read, replay and self-directed pace. When an explanation is requested, make it optional and return to the passage. Do not turn every difficult thought into a test or a lecture.

**C14 One meaningful focus.** Find the governing relationship of the communication. Sequence material so the recipient can follow: orientation, meaning, important detail, evidence, next choice where relevant. Avoid repeated headings, labels, paragraphs and summaries that add reading work. Keep a longer form when depth or artistic intent requires it.

**C15 Copy earns its place.** Interface words reduce uncertainty, show understanding, request missing information, orient, communicate state or boundary, offer a choice, report evidence or hand back responsibility. Marketing words express a truthful specific promise. Artistic language may create feeling or atmosphere. A word count is not a universal quality measure.

**C16 Authored voice and brand promise.** Establish who speaks, to whom, in what situation, about which real value, and with what evidence. Choose a voice appropriate to that relationship. Specificity, rhythm and material experience matter more than a clever tagline. Respect each product's own identity; no copied celebrity voice, borrowed brand slogans or universal DAWT skin.

**C17 Storytelling as comprehension.** Use the story, example, sequence, contrast or metaphor to reveal a real relationship. Introduce what matters early where appropriate. Do not fabricate anecdotes, testimonials, metrics or emotional arcs; do not turn a helpful explanation into an engagement funnel. A hook should earn the recipient's attention, not exploit it.

**C18 Anti-slop is accountability.** Detect generic promises, interchangeable headlines, assistant filler, forced contrasts, overexplaining, fake profundity, manufactured urgency, unsupported certainty and decorative labels. These are diagnostic signals, not general proof of AI authorship or a universal banned-word list. Retain stricter product-specific prohibitions. Ask whether a named editor can defend the actual words.

**C19 Editorial judgement and creative divergence.** When an expression is generic, return to the human encounter and source material. Recover relevant writing, spoken cadence, real objects, cultural contexts and prior human corrections. Explore a few different meaningful approaches only when the decision warrants it; select, subtract, refine and test. Do not replace predictable AI prose with arbitrary novelty, forced slang or another stock voice.

**C20 Honest limits and safe recovery.** Say what was not found, checked, supported or completed; offer the smallest useful recovery path without blaming the person. Do not claim material is still held unless retention is established. Keep the source available where authorised. No silent provider substitution, hidden monitoring or irreversible action.

**C21 Evidence and independent reception.** Evaluate what the recipient actually understands, remembers where relevant, expresses and can do independently. Compare the ordinary alternative; test uncertainty, corrections, language and physical reading/listening conditions. A screenshot, syntactic lint, model self-review or AI-origin detector cannot establish communication excellence.

**C22 Accumulation and restraint.** Preserve useful source-linked observations, corrections, mechanisms, tested applicability, limitations and human decisions. Reuse existing capability and minimum relevant context. Never load every archive by default. Do not turn a saved reference into doctrine, a design cue into consent, or reading a foundation into execution authority.

## 3. Living editorial craft — how to work

### Establish the communication job
Record audience, author/speaker, medium, situation, actual source, intended outcome, protected facts/voice, uncertainty, current alternatives, and where authority stops. Choose the operation (e.g. translate vs rewrite) explicitly. If a phrase carries a consequence, identify its supporting evidence before editing.

### Author the content
Choose a governing relationship instead of a generic format. Useful structures, not compulsory templates: one clear point supported by reason and example; a source's claim followed by verified meaning; a story that moves from problem to changed understanding; one request with exact condition and owner; a script shaped into meaningful breath/performable beats. Write with concrete verbs, natural sentence rhythm and useful detail. Remove repetition, abstract filler and performative reassurance. Put the important information where a tired recipient can find it. Let the author select degree of editing and language/register.

### Edit in passes
1. **Truth edit:** compare original/derived content, speakers, facts, time, conditionality, commitments and omissions. Flag unsupported inferences.
2. **Meaning edit:** ask whether the recipient can state the point and any necessary action. Repair misleading structure before polishing sentences.
3. **Voice edit:** preserve author stance and meaningful idiom; adjust degree of formality only as requested.
4. **Economy edit:** remove words, labels, and sections that do no work; retain essential complexity, useful cadence and context.
5. **Medium edit:** read aloud; compare spoken vs visual requirements; mark optional pauses/emphasis; check length, wrapping, text scaling, pronunciation, turn ownership and non-audio path.
6. **Reality edit:** check affordance, status, source, permission and consequence against actual operation. Verify destinations independently where relevant.
7. **Independent reception:** test an actual reader/listener in appropriate context. Retain failures; revise a specific cause rather than regenerate indefinitely.

No need for seven model invocations: these are editorial questions, not an agent chain. Use deterministic diff/checks for exact values where sufficient, one human or model review for semantic differences, and independent judgement for consequential cases.

## 6. Language Yard of Excellence — review and proof

Hard gates (must pass where applicable): original/derived and speaker attribution correct; no critical meaning/condition/negation/commitment errors; no fabricated source or state; no unauthorised disclosure, sending or action; accessibility of critical meaning/controls; safe correction, exit and no protected-fixture regression. Missing evidence is NOT TESTED, not PASS.

Review independent dimensions:
- Accuracy and communicative force: compare source and derivative proposition by proposition, identify additions, omissions, altered modality and ambiguous entities.
- Comprehension and agency: can the person describe the situation and the available choice without unnecessary help? Can they correct, decline and leave?
- Authorial/cultural fit: recognisable voice, appropriate register and specific context; no automatic homogenisation or accent bias.
- Editorial economy and craft: useful hierarchy, concrete wording, cadence, purposeful imagery; delete, swap, specificity and read-aloud tests; no cosmetic AI-detector proxy.
- Speech/reading experience: actual microphone/playback truth, intelligibility, natural pause, accessibility, correct turn yield, text fallback and recoverable place.
- Reliability: source availability/freshness, safe abstention, latency, costs, repeat encounter and measured correction burden.
- Evidence/acceptance: PASS / PARTIAL / FAIL / BLOCKED / NOT TESTED per dimension with actual evidence; human ACCEPT / REVISE / REJECT / HOLD remains separate. Preserve comparison limits.

Do not set universal numeric aesthetic gates or claim a comprehension improvement without observation. Use the existing Flow study/owner material only within current authority; no new experiment admitted by this foundation.

## 7. Worker orientation and continuity

Before new communication work, read the applicable foundation and product owner source; recover current product lock, source, rights and prior human correction. Return: HUMAN / INTENTION / AUDIENCE / SOURCE / OPERATION / PROTECT / QUESTION / EDITORIAL MOVE / PROOF / AUTHORITY-STOP. Choose the smallest meaningful edit. For authorised build, inspect actual strings/audio/outputs and test real states and failure conditions, not only a style guide. Preserve original and actual evidence, not just a fluent final. No global prompt, new model, fine-tune, model filter, voice clone or new service is installed or authorised.

Maintain source records where they already live. Transcribe stores source passages; the Obsidian collaboration stores reasoned relevance links; Immich holds visual material; Gallery supports selective comparison; Quiet Reader supports reception; Workroom carries accepted undertakings. Don't conflate relevance with same-source identity. Local source atlas supplies selected records with dates, passages and provenance; no private raw text, audio or image published here. Source indexing is a navigation aid, not a claim of source truth or consent. Reopen original before material use; differentiate inspected, reported, inferred and unverified.
