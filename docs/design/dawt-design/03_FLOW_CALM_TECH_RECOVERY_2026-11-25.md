- QuietTile historically has *distinct* strands: an early breathing/ritual experiment, and later Quiet Tile Room 001 investigating environmental light, sound and a non-demanding room surface. Do not merge them or infer mood surveillance.
- Quiet Reader's original 14 June product spec calls for exit dignity, silence as a reading surface, chosen-source dominance, readable structure and exact return—**reception, not consumption**. Its later implementation reports need current revalidation, not blanket PASS.
- Quiet Runner recorded technical audio playback success but failed the human encounter; its revised contract investigates **WARRANT → CLAIM → EXPRESSION → VOICE → YIELD**. The activity can continue without an utterance.
- HIGH PURPOSE investigates holding unresolved material without a forced task label or dashboard; DAWT Design's provisional grammar demands deliberate attention, earned motion, controlled density and distinct product identities. Therapeutic does not mean pastel.
- Transcribe and Workroom investigate return without reconstruction, but deliberate source preservation must not turn into passive monitoring.
- Flow's already shared [Acoustic Turn Ownership](https://github.com/muntakabasit/flow/issues/1#issuecomment-7075902985) and [Situational Care Design Yard](https://github.com/muntakabasit/flow/issues/1#issuecomment-7166189178) are especially relevant. **Design challenge for Flow:** Test one real turn or controlled synthetic change against four outcomes: necessary cue; unnecessary cue suppressed; uncertainty disclosed; human control restored. Count attention stolen, false and missed interventions, resumption, sensory costs and actual truth/permission violations. A beautiful sound or token saving does not compensate for a misleading state or a human becoming the system's dispatcher.
