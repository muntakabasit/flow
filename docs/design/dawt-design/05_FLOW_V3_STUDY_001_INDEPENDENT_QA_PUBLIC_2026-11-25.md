## Seven quality findings

**Q1 — Human acceptance untested (hard gate).** No actual iPhone encounter, tired/one-handed trial, independent judge, anti-slop critique, or full Excellence Check had been completed. Explanation fidelity, false-completion and second-encounter transfer remained PARTIAL. A developer screenshot and clean console do not establish a user benefit.

**Q2 — The human-burden hypothesis remains unproved.** An original-first annotated reading page may make the person scan, tap and interpret multiple layers, while the meaningful obligation appears only after the source. Count steps, rereads, comprehension errors and time to a correct understanding against an honest plain baseline rather than assuming an annotation is an improvement.

**Q3 — Original speaker attribution fails in the transfer fixture.** The second source alternates quoted human material with a responding institution. The design makes that authorship distinction clear only through additional explanatory interaction, not consistently at the location of the source. This threatens source truth.

**Q4 — Interactive text is too difficult to target without demonstrated accessibility.** Source inspection found inline interactive spans without a reliable minimum touch-target geometry. Keyboard handling and focus outlines do not, by themselves, establish one-handed operation with varied text sizes or a screen reader.

**Q5 — Basic visibility-state bugs were discovered late.** A hidden panel and the supposedly hidden baseline source initially remained visible due to a display override; the change was then corrected. The fix is positive, but the late discovery reveals incomplete early behavioural validation.

**Q6 — The visual mechanism is not yet distinguishably Flow.** A restrained serif/sans message with dotted annotations can be defensible, but no real evidence yet establishes that it yields a recognisable, authored comprehension experience rather than a conventional annotation reader. A novel palette is **not** the remedy; prove that composition clarifies the undertaking.

**Q7 — Comparison and publication standing are incomplete.** The candidate and baseline differ in both presentation and authored explanatory structure. Explanations were self-checked by their generating model. A private hosted link does not prove a confidentiality guarantee; content-specific hosting permission cannot be generalised to GitHub.

## Fundamental reframing: first encounter before first task

The person was already inside a prepared reading fixture when Study 001 began. It therefore could not answer why a new person opens Flow, what help they recognise, how they bring a source, what permissions apply, or whether the first result changes their understanding.

The subsequent [First Encounter / Onboarding inquiry](04_FLOW_FIRST_ENCOUNTER_ONBOARDING_2026-11-25.md) is a prerequisite **design research direction**, not an approved new interface or change to the human-directed product lock.

## Fair correction order

1. **Preserve source fidelity and human authorship:** clear speakers, claims, dates, exceptions, uncertainty and responsibility at the point of encounter.
2. **Make the first useful understanding easy to recognise:** test it against the plain baseline while preserving the complete original and correction route.
3. **Reduce actual human work:** count transfers, scanning, taps, readbacks, corrections and uncertain conclusions.
4. **Test on the real device:** iPhone 13, ordinary one-handed use, screen reader, text scaling and honest failure state where applicable.
5. **Obtain an independent Yard and human verdict:** real test evidence, not solely model self-review or emulator output.
6. **Separate gates:** an explicitly authorised **controlled human design test** may reach the tester while still clearly marked NOT ACCEPTED; acceptance-for-use requires a separate verdict. A policy that demands a user's test before they may view the study needs this distinction.

Do not silently redesign the second transfer fixture, widen the study into a universal assistant, introduce account access, alter the protected interpreter runtime, or ship cosmetic fixes as proof of comprehension.
