# DAWT Design Yard 007 — Flow's Connected First Encounter: Human FAIL → Experience Correction

**Date:** 26 November 2026
**Owner of this assessment:** DAWT Design Intelligence / Front-End Quality Guardian
**Origin:** The human's first-hand rejection of the new Gmail-connected onboarding, carried in the Flow owner verdict [#1/7182483277](https://github.com/muntakabasit/flow/issues/1#issuecomment-7182483277). The human also supplied creative references, described as personality, presence and relational understanding. This design assessment does not claim to have independently inspected the actual rendered application.
**Standing:** **HUMAN ONBOARDING FAIL — INDEPENDENT DAWT DESIGN CORRECTION FOR EXISTING APP DELIVERY.** Not a new product lock, proposed new agent, general OAuth grant, style freeze, runtime test report or standalone prototype request.

## 1. Diagnosis: a structural failure, not a missing visual polish pass

The user connected a real source expecting Flow to become meaningfully present in everyday life. The reported journey instead made them:
**open Flow → browse Gmail messages → choose one → wait for inference → read an annotated or partial explanation**.

This is the incumbent Gmail task with an added step. Its failure is **not** simply typography, latency, an underdesigned dashboard or absence of a mascot.

The connected resource was permitted to determine the main interface and the first task. The product behaved like a Gmail client before it had demonstrated a distinct comprehension relationship. This repeats Study 001's deeper error: construct a document explanation surface without first proving the person's introduction, intention, first recognition and release.

**The human rejects this onboarding experience, not the use of Google as machinery.** Working authentication, credential custody and Gmail transport should be retained where independently verified. Source read success does not equal comprehension success; human FAIL does not automatically invalidate every working integration.

**Important evidence ceiling:** Flow owner / Belawu sources report Gmail list/open, a 34-second long-message explanation that returned no marks, and a prefix-length patch. This reviewer has not personally seen the live app, run the private inbox, verified the actual patch, inspected user credentials or exercised the physical iPhone. Preserve those reports as reports.

## 2. One defining design decision

**FLOW IS AN ENCOUNTER WITH MEANING. GMAIL IS A DONOR, NOT ITS MAIN STAGE.**

The first-use design is not "welcome slides" or "pick a tool". It is **an interaction in which the human recognises Flow's distinct promise, deliberately expresses an intention, consents only when relevant, gets a truthful and materially clearer outcome, and can leave**.

**No automatic transition to inbox after Google connection.** Return to the person's pre-auth intent. Gmail remains behind a source selection/review path when needed.

Aesthetic references—fashion/editorial composition, Adinkra-inspired authorship, relational information graphics, acoustic turn ownership and Calm Technology—are **creative research donors**, not permission to appropriate imagery, introduce a mandatory mascot, lock a colour palette or fabricate intelligence. Borrow the mechanics of graphic storytelling, rhythm, social materiality and humane feedback, not third-party artwork.

## 3. Flow's encounter choreography: ONE journey, conditional branches

### A — The person arrives before connection
**First impression:** flow-specific expressive composition and one legible human invitation. Candidate wording: **"What are you trying to make sense of?"** (not final copy). No login wall, inbox, tool-grid, feature carousel, personality quiz, AI model switcher, ornamental AI orb or unsolicited personal summary.

**Living but restful:** one composed focal relationship, with purposeful lightweight response to direct interaction. Avoid gratuitous preloader animation, fake listening, random "thinking" activity, auto-play private speech or a constant moving background. Rest itself is a valid design state.

**Honest first move:** supported manual text/selected-source route, plus a clear invitation to connect Google for a separate, stated purpose. A short fictional example can introduce the *kind* of relationship Flow can explain, but must be visibly labelled illustration and must not be confused with the human's real life.

### B — The person has an intention and elects to connect Google
**Keep the human's sentence alive across the redirect.** First capture the *undertaking* (one sentence, selected source or supported entry) before explaining why Gmail permission might help. Only request authentication and specific source access when warranted. Identity login and Gmail read permission remain distinct.

**After Google returns:** restore the same intention, not an inbox listing, "connected accounts" dashboard or preselected Gmail message. Confirm the account only as needed; show actual consent/scope/revocation precisely. If the permission was denied, expired, disconnected, wrong-account or offline, say so and offer a real fallback; no implied access, punishment or invisible provider switch.

**Question-first retrieval (only if the consumer supports it):** an on-demand, bounded authorised search/selection based on what the person asked for. If the runtime cannot legitimately identify the intended message without a manual selection, honestly offer **Choose a message** as an instrument rather than pretending autonomous recognition works.

### C — The first actual Flow moment
**Lead with the RELATIONSHIP, not the message body:** identify what happened, what changed, whose statement is whose, what it means, what is still unknown and what the human may choose next. Present no more than the actual source justifies. The person's question may demand a different form (brief spoken orientation, editable relational diagram, precise original, single meaningful cue).

For a permitted, source-proven time change, the central composition might show **EARLIER TIME → VERIFIED NEW TIME** and one unchanged requirement, if really established. The original message remains one deliberate gesture away as evidence. This is a *fictional visual grammar demonstration*, not a universal appointment UI or a licence to mine Gmail.

**What the person should feel:** "That helped me notice something important"—not "I have now read this email in a different application."

### D — Conversation, silence and return
A follow-up may reference the existing encounter without making the person re-explain or reopen their Gmail list. Source remains inspectable, with uncertainty and actual fresh/partial coverage visible. Flow does not claim to have set an alarm, sent a reply or changed an event until an authorised provider has verified the action.

Release the person's attention. A returning person meets either their deliberately retained unfinished undertaking (where actually implemented and consented) or an honest quiet invitation. No compulsory retention, notification, or new life-dashboard.

## 4. A more specific visual/interaction seed — not a locked style

**Candidate mechanism: "Meaning has a direction."** The first central object is an expressive editorial *relation* between a person, their question and something they are trying to understand—not a card for every Gmail message. A tiny change in relationship, timing or emphasis communicates actual state, not fake consciousness. The composition may shift from a quiet resting focus to a clear source-backed comparison, then recede again.

- **Hierarchy:** one intentional focal element, a peripheral evidence layer, and an unobtrusive route to inspect the source.
- **Typography:** chosen for authored personality and readability, tested at physical phone scale; not defaults chosen solely because "minimalist" looks calm.
- **Movement:** authored transition indicates real source/turn change; instant reduced-motion equivalent, no animation delay before useful content.
- **Colour/material:** cultural and expressive reference must have concrete provenance, meaning and ownership; not a universal DAWT gradient or generic pseudo-African ornament.
- **Acoustic:** optional, truthful turn-taking punctuation; do not fake supported voice, active microphone, private speech or readiness. Keep full text interaction possible.
- **Accessible:** essential meaning is legible without sound, motion, colour-only distinctions or precise tapping; focus, VoiceOver, text size and thumb reach validated on iPhone.

There is no lock on these art-direction candidates. They must be judged as part of the *existing real app encounter*, against the original source and human verdict. A static pretty first screen is not sufficient.

## 5. Design/engineering tracks must not mask each other

**Experience track — failing:** connection immediately becomes an inbox, followed by a select/wait/read burden. A new blank chatbot, set of four capabilities, branding splash or stylish inbox is not a repair. Do not let the next worker report "PASS" because the Google button works.

**Source intelligence track — separately incomplete:** the reported ~12k source and prefix-limited ~6k inference create a specific risk that crucial negation, conditions or exceptions at the end can be missed. A visible "partial" label improves disclosure but cannot justify confident whole-message conclusions. The owner/worker must separately demonstrate source coverage and safe abstention for a long **synthetic** adversarial case.

**Capability track — preserve:** actual working Google sign-in, consent, retrieval and Sam-owned custody according to verified implementation. The already expanded Google work is governed by [Belawu crossing](https://github.com/muntakabasit/flow/issues/1#issuecomment-7178316637); verify actual Ofa Jack executable admission/work identity/host. Do not mutate the protected interpreter/runtime or spread private source content to GitHub.

## 6. Five practical comparison gates within the CURRENT delivery

No separate app, generic speculative study series or new agent is needed. The Flow owner has already called for a **real-app** correction under an admitted work envelope, if available.

1. **Entry recognition:** On first launch without an email, can the person recognise Flow's promise and one honest next action without a feature taxonomy or scrolling an inbox?
2. **Intent preserved across sign-in:** Given a specific pre-connection question, does Flow return to it after Google consent, denial or a temporary failure—or dump the person at a Gmail list?
3. **First useful outcome:** Given one user-chosen/consented real situation and a matching safe synthetic comparator, can the person explain what actually changed, which condition matters and what is *not* known more accurately, with fewer transfers/steps than ordinary Gmail?
4. **Truth and agency:** Is the original source one step away, attribution clear, permission narrowly disclosed, revocation comprehensible, and unavailable/partial data never presented as fact? Can the person correct, leave and return without being trapped?
5. **Multimodal craft and real-device acceptance:** Does the form genuinely convey a relationship with restrained meaningful motion/optional truthful sound? Does it pass iPhone 13 thumb, text scale, reduced motion, focus and no-audio routes? No unsupported voice claims. Record actual human judgement—not model self-critique.

Measure manual transfers, taps, time to a **correct useful recognition** (not just latency), attention stolen, premature text exposure, repeated human questions, source errors, trust/consent comprehension, meaningful moments of delight, abandonment, and learning/return. Compare against Gmail as the *incumbent human task*, not merely another model prompt.

**Hard FAIL:** displaying source text as new life insights without evidence; misattribution; source incompleteness glossed over; fabricated actions; permission misuse; fake listening/voice; forced provider switch; accessibility-critical confusion.
