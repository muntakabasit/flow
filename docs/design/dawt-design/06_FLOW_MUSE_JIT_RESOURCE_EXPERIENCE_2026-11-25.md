## DAWT DESIGN YARD — Flow × Muse | Just-in-Time Resource Experience 001 (25 Nov 2026)

**FROM:** DAWT Design Intelligence / Front-End Quality Guardian. **SOURCE:** Human-supplied "DAWT × Flow × Muse — The Just-in-Time Resource Principle" (one source, not independent evidence from three teams). **STANDING:** ORIGINAL DESIGN RESEARCH RETURN / FIRST-ENCOUNTER DESIGN CANDIDATE. **NO BUILD, CONNECTOR ACCESS, GMAIL WATCH, PURCHASE, ALARM, ACCOUNT ACTION, NEW AGENT, PRODUCT SCOPE CHANGE OR RELEASE.**

### 1. Distinctive design contribution
**Retain:** "Own the relationship and the intelligence of coordination. Borrow the machinery. Preserve human authority."

**Mutate for the actual encounter:** The human should not choose a model, capability, agent or provider. They should present an undertaking, get the smallest useful source-grounded result, understand what the system is entitled to do, and receive an honest completion status. **Prepared / Situational / Immediate are backstage policies, NOT a three-mode opening dashboard.**

This extends the [DAWT Design first-encounter correction](https://github.com/muntakabasit/flow/issues/1#issuecomment-7171804466): Study 001 started after the person supplied a message, without proving why they would enter Flow, how source consent works, or whether the result reduced human work. JIT is NOT a licence to skip that proof.

### 2. Four distinct truths in the UI
- **AVAILABLE:** a provider or plugin advertises this operation.
- **READY:** the exact deployed DAWT runtime + device has a tested route.
- **AUTHORISED:** the user approved the specific source/scope/time/purpose; a connected ChatGPT plugin or Gmail sign-in is not Flow permission.
- **CONFIRMED:** the provider returned inspectable completion evidence. A drafted reply is not sent, a prepared alarm is not set, an estimated departure is not a confirmed schedule, and a basket is not purchased.

At each step, keep source/version/freshness, uncertainty, next human decision, retry/revoke and a proper none/unsupported/stale/permission-denied state. Never switch providers after denial silently. No celebratory completed styling before provider confirmation. A correct quiet/no-action outcome is a success.

### 3. Candidate first-use scene (illustrative, not operational)
A person says **"Help me get ready for tomorrow."**

Without account permissions, the honest first move is to invite the smallest selected source or use existing user-provided context—**not** fabricate a prepared agenda.

With separately proven read-only routes, Flow may show one relevant appointment with provider/source timestamp; a relevant document with source/version; a departure estimate with assumptions; and a clearly **not-set** alarm awaiting a supported device action and approval. Display only what the situation actually requires, not fixed four-card furniture. Offer review / ask / dismiss, then leave attention alone.

Do not require the person to manage Muse, Sam, Flow, Drive, Clock or a connector stack. Existing owner boundaries continue.

### 5. Test the experience separately from technical contracts
**A — HUMAN FIRST ENCOUNTER:** one consented real task on iPhone 13; compare manually selected source vs clearly hypothetical prepared read-only situation. Record correct understanding, source/permission comprehension, steps/transfers/corrections, trust, misleading completion cues, interruption, exit and re-entry. Existing Flow policy requires real approved material for human experience acceptance; synthetic material alone cannot give it a PASS.

**B — ENGINEERING REVIEW CANDIDATE:** the human proposes one synthetic day with appointment/email, document, alarm and product comparison. Under separately approved Ofa Jack owner envelope only, prove capability readiness, minimal source envelope, explicit approval crossings, truthful unsupported states, verified provider receipt, and second-task reuse without rebuilding machinery. No real accounts required or authorised.

Hard FAIL: unauthorised access/action, fabricated completion, source/condition error, misleading verified status. Human administrative burden is measured alongside tokens/cost.
